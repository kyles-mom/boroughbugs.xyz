Here you can watch the episodes of Borough Bugs which are currently out!


Series 1, Episode 1 - WESTMINSTER

Zach and Vinbee begin hosting a show talking about the London Boroughs, and start off with Westminster, detailing most of the famous tourist areas in Central London.


Series 1, Episode 2 - CAMDEN

Zach finds out that Vinbee doesn't eat breakfast, which leads him to speak about Camden Market, along with its great amount of shops and stalls serving food from all over the world.


Series 1, Episode 3 - KENSINGTON & CHELSEA

Zach and Vinbee receive a note from an apparent fan, spawning a discussion about how you can't Zach without Vinbee, just like how you can't have Kensington without Chelsea!


Series 1, Episode 4 - HAMMERSMITH & FULHAM

Vinbee's running late, leaving Zach to run the episode on his own, which he uses as an opportunity to talk about how Hammersmith & Fulham didn't always have Fulham in its name.


Series 1, Episode 5 - SOUTHWARK

After Zach is found reciting a line from one of Shakespeare's plays, he and Vinbee begin to talk about the famous theatre in the north of Southwark, Shakespeare's globe, or rather, the replica.


Series 1, Episode 6 - WANDSWORTH

Zach and Vinbee discuss the various redevelopments some parts of London have had, including Wandsworth's Battersea Power Station, where the redevelopment has brought the area to life!


Series 1, Episode 7 - LAMBETH

Zach and Vinbee have a staring contest, to which neither of them win because bugs cannot blink, and where there's eyes, there's the London Eye, on the South Bank in Lambeth!


Series 1, Episode 8 - LEWISHAM

Vera invites their friend from Lewisham to come onto Borough Bugs. Dianthus, a cyan stag beetle, joins Zach and Vinbee's discussion of the borough of Lewisham.
