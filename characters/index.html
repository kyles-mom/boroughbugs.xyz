Who are the bugs? This page will tell you who they are!


ZACH

he/they - voiced by Jack A. B.

Zach is a blue monarch butterfly, he's the main face of Borough Bugs, and his own YouTube channel! Originally from London, Zach is a fun, easy-going insect, although sometimes rather cocky and arrogant, one of his favourite things about London would have to be what he calls "the obscure areas", he loves going to places that most people may not have heard of, as it's a great place to get some peace and quiet. Zach's often self-conscious about just how tiny he is, he's only about 4 inches tall! Nonetheless, Zach loves London, and wouldn't do anything to leave it. He also has incredible skills at playing the drums, you honestly would if you had four arms! Zach's drumkit is currently owned by Lúcás, who has refused to give it back to him.


VINBEE

she/they - was voiced by Vinnie B.

Vinbee is a pink white-tailed bumblebee, and is the other main face of Borough Bugs, she is a huge city lover, especially for cities from where the comes from, the Midlands and the North. Vinbee first met Zach when she made a vlog about a trip she took to London, so Zach got in touch with her, and the rest is history! Fun fact, Vinbee's actually trans, the skirt kind of gives it away! She also has a tendency to skip breakfast, and as we all know, breakfast is essential to your day! This doesn't mean she ALWAYS skips it, though, she will eat breakfast from time to time, and obviously eats throughout the day, she's not starving herself, guys.


LÚCÁS

he/him - voiced by MixelsFan2015

Lúcás is a purple firefly, more specifically, the common European glowworm. Hailing from Ireland, Lúcás is the shortest insect out of the main four, and sometimes forgets that he can fly, he's also currently in possession of Zach's drumkit, which he lent to Lúcás to help him practice playing the drums, he only learnt how to play the ever famous "Joke Rimshot", or as most people know it, the "ba dum tss" sound, and he never gave it back to Zach. He's often annoyed about being left out of the show, and wishes he could play a bigger role in Borough Bugs, but until that time comes, he'll be stuck off to the side, playing Zach's drums.


VERA

they/them - voiced by Oliver A. "Clover"

Vera is a lime hawk-moth, and they're non-binary! Vera is the only one out of the main four to have normal looking eyes, giving them loads more emotion in their face! Vera is named after the aloe vera plant, hence their green hue. They also have a fascination with lamps, especially if the bulb has warm lighting! Because of this, Vera spends most of their time studying colour theory, and is an artist, while we have yet to see any of their art, from what I've heard, it's "simply marvellous"! Despite being the tallest bug out of the main four, Vera is rather shy, somewhat a "gentle giant", if you may.
